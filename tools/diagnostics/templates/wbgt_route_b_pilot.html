What these numbers mean

Daily outdoor WBGT is calculated per climate cell. Annual counts, means and maxima are then area-averaged to each district or block. Fractional day counts are expected: they describe the area's average cell exposure, not days when the entire district exceeded the threshold.

This pilot uses CarbonPlan's shade → QDM correction → sun adjustment chain adapted to IRT geometry. Calibration: 1985–2014. Historical reporting: 1990–2010. Future correction windows: 2020–2039, 2040–2059 and 2060–2080. Annual metrics require complete 365-day cell-years, excluding February 29.

Coverage and interpretation limits

630 of 656 places meet the 95% retained-area screen. The 26 excluded places are in Kerala; missing data stay missing. Resolution remains that of the underlying 0.25° climate grid, so neighbouring blocks can share similar values.

UHE-Daily is a provisional shade reference, not independent outdoor-WBGT observations. Seasonal held-out performance improves, but Rajasthan's ≥32 °C count errors worsen. Future application-window choices materially affect some annual counts. This single-model pilot does not express ensemble uncertainty and has not changed production metrics or composite scores.

CMCC calculations were stopped by user choice; partial second-model results are excluded. The regional-first comparator remains diagnostic evidence outside this viewer.
